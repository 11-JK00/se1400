Portfolio Website for KRNP (Kaleb Rudy Nogalas Pictures)
My friend Kaleb is a small‑time photographer and needs a website to display his best works.

Competitors

Shailendra Rana

  • Uses an online website builder.
  • Clean UI but lacks unique characteristics.
  • Social media handles placed at the top of the page.
  • No clear border separating the header and footer from the main content.
  • Each gallery has a title and description; images displayed using inline‑block.
  • Linked pages include:
    • Fashion catalogues, editorials, commercial campaigns
    • Portrait, travel, and fashion categories
    • About and Contact pages
  • Each gallery opens in a new tab.
  • Color scheme:
    1. Heading – black
    2. Text – grey
    3. Page/body – white

Will Bremridge

  • Minimal text descriptions.
  • Latest work showcased on the homepage.
  • Grid of high‑quality images.
  • Clean, minimalist layout.
  • No clear border separating header and footer from main content.
  • Social media links at the top of the page.
  • Images fade in as you scroll.
  • Color scheme:
    1. Off‑white background
    2. Black text
    3. Highlighter red for titles
  • Pages included:
    • Contacts
    • About

Olga Miljko

  • Header contains navigation links, website title, Instagram link, and a quotation button.
  • Footer includes additional social media and contact details.
  • Images are stacked using flexbox.
  • Fashion‑focused portfolio.

Summary

To sum things up these website have a clean easy to navigate UI and provide quick links to their social-media accounts which and make it easy for people to contact them as per they provide their social media and contacts right at the header which make sense as most photographer tend to put their work on social media. However a few of these websites lack contrast in both color and text style. I will try to adapt the clean design style of these websites and avoid the blend color schemes and use different color and font styles.
